# Customer Support SLA Policy

The current operating target for standard-priority tickets is a first response within 60 minutes during supported hours. High-priority production incidents have a 15-minute first-response target.

The SLA is measured on median performance and breach rate. Teams should not optimize median response time alone if doing so reduces investigation quality or increases repeat contacts.

Operational reviews should prioritize SLA breaches, unresolved customer impact, and repeatable sources of ticket volume.
