# Support Automation Study

The automation pilot focused on password resets, invoice-copy requests, and basic account-status questions. These categories had clear resolution rules and low operational risk.

Automated handling reduced manual touches by 31 percent in the pilot group. Cases involving billing disputes, security concerns, data deletion, or ambiguous customer intent were routed to human agents.

The pilot recommends expanding automation only when a category has stable decision rules, measurable quality checks, and a clear escalation path.
