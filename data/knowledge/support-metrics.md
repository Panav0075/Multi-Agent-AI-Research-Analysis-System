# Support Operations Metrics

During the latest eight-week review, the support team handled an average of 4,800 tickets per month. Median first-response time was 42 minutes, compared with the current SLA target of 60 minutes.

Billing questions, password-reset questions, and API configuration questions represented 46 percent of incoming tickets. Categories with complete troubleshooting articles had a 21 percent lower repeat-contact rate than categories without maintained documentation.

Escalated engineering tickets had a median resolution time of 19 hours. Reducing unnecessary escalations is therefore more valuable than reducing a few minutes from already compliant first-response performance.
