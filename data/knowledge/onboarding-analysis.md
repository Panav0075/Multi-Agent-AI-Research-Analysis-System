# Customer Onboarding Analysis

A review of 320 customer onboarding workflows found that account provisioning, standard workspace configuration, and welcome-email preparation were highly repetitive. Together these activities consumed approximately 28 percent of onboarding specialist time.

Security reviews, data-migration planning, and custom access decisions had much higher variation and frequently required customer-specific judgment.

The analysis recommends automating standard provisioning and communication steps while keeping human approval for security exceptions, migration decisions, and unusual permission requests.
