# Knowledge Base Coverage Study

The documentation review found that 62 percent of the twenty highest-volume support questions had a complete self-service article. Eight high-volume issue categories either had outdated instructions or no dedicated article.

When customers viewed a complete troubleshooting article before opening a ticket, 34 percent did not continue to ticket creation. For customers who still contacted support after reading an article, the initial ticket contained more diagnostic information and required fewer clarification messages.

The study recommends improving articles for the highest-volume repeatable issues before expanding documentation into low-frequency edge cases.
